Course objectives

  • Understand fundamental concepts, architecture and functions of operating systems.
  • Study process management, memory management, file systems and I/O systems.
  • Analyze synchronization, deadlock and resource-allocation strategies.
  • Explore modern OS design principles and case studies (e.g. Linux, Windows).

Course policies

  • Bring your own calculator, notebook and pen; do not borrow in class.
  • Late submission of assignments carries a zero mark; the average of assignment marks counts.
  • At least three class tests, with or without announcement; the best two count. No make-up tests; missing a test without notice gives zero.
  • Midterm and final are closed book and closed notes; mobile phones are prohibited in the exam hall; bring a non-smart watch.
  • Laptops and phones are strongly discouraged during class.
